### 3. Historical Context

Sam Altman's journey in the tech world began early. He dropped out of Stanford University to co-found Loopt in 2005, which participated in the first batch of Y Combinator. After Loopt's acquisition, he became a partner at Y Combinator in 2011 and was appointed its president in 2014, transforming it into a much larger and more influential institution.

In 2015, Altman co-founded OpenAI with Elon Musk and others, initially as a non-profit dedicated to ensuring artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. He left Y Combinator in 2019 to fully dedicate himself to OpenAI as its CEO. Under his **leadership**, OpenAI transitioned to a "capped-profit" model to attract the significant capital required for advanced AI research, culminating in major breakthroughs like GPT-3, DALL-E, and ChatGPT. His tenure at OpenAI was briefly interrupted in November 2023 when he was dismissed by the board, only to be reinstated days later following widespread employee and investor pressure, underscoring his critical role and influence within the organization.
